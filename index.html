Research

研究方向

My research concerns the design, deployment, and empirical evaluation of agentic systems built on large language models. It is organised around one question:

When an autonomous system takes on part of a user's cognitive work, how should it be architected, instrumented, and governed so that the user keeps agency over the task?

I

System architecture for agentic AI

Multi-agent LLM systems in which task planning, adaptive feedback, and reflective prompting are separate, cooperating components; orchestration policies that decide how much of a task the system performs; retrieval-augmented grounding; and on-premise inference for institutions with data-sovereignty constraints.

II

Instrumentation and data infrastructure

Multimodal data lakes that combine dialogue text, speech, behavioural logs, and wearable physiological signals; automated semantic classification of interaction traces at scale; de-identification, consent management, and ethical reuse of data collected in operational systems.

III

Evaluation of user and organisational effects

Quasi-experimental and large-sample observational designs; mixed-effects modelling of nested interaction logs; adoption of generative AI across disciplines; and AI literacy and governance as conditions for responsible use.

Uedu Academy

雙語 AI 學習平台

Uedu is an agentic AI learning platform I co-founded in 2022 and develop with Prof. Chia-Kai Chang at National Central University. It is a production service deployed at ten universities and indexing more than 31,000 courses, and a research instrument that produces de-identified multimodal interaction data under an IRB-approved governance framework.

  • 10universities deployed
  • 31,000+courses indexed
  • BronzeTechnology Innovation Award, eLFA 2026
AGENT 1

Learner Profile

Keeps a running picture of each learner's level, goals, and history.

AGENT 2

Task Planning

Breaks course goals into the next task the learner can manage.

AGENT 3

Adaptive Feedback

Responds with Socratic prompts under a scaffolding-and-fading policy.

AGENT 4

Self-Reflection

Asks learners to review what they did and plan the next step.

Reflection feeds back into the learner profile, closing the loop.

Research infrastructure

Data

Educational omics data lake

Six data streams (cognitive, linguistic, physiological–neural, social, environmental, ethical) under one de-identified, IRB-governed schema, released to external researchers under controlled access.

Sensing

PALM

Physiologically-aware language-model tutoring: consumer wearables, through a research partnership with Garmin Taiwan, aligned with LLM tutoring for affect-aware adaptation.

Inference

On-premise model service

A campus-hosted, Traditional-Chinese-optimised model with transparent load telemetry, for institutions with data-residency constraints.

Access

Developer API and MCP server

A public API, a Model Context Protocol server, retrieval-augmented knowledge bases, and containerised computational services.
